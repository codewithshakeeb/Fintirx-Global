COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT

Director Identity Verification

Under the UK Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act (ECCTA), Companies House requires all company directors and people with significant control (PSCs) to verify their identity.

We guide you through each step of the verification process, ensuring your passport and proof of address meet Companies House standards so your incorporation proceeds without administrative rejection.

LEGAL OFFICE PRESENCE

Official UK Registered Office

A UK company must have an official physical address in the UK. This address appears on the public Companies House register and receives official correspondence from HMRC, Courts, and Companies House.

We provide an address service in London, scan official government mail daily, and notify you instantly via email. The term is 12 full months from registration, with subsequent renewal clearly stated in your itemized quote.
