SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON

US LLC vs. UK LTD at a Glance

Both give you a separate legal company with limited liability. Compare key parameters to find the right fit.

Parameter US LLC UK LTD
Registered with A US state (e.g., Wyoming, Delaware, Florida) Companies House
Owners are called Members Shareholders
Run by Members or managers Directors
Local address needed A registered agent in the state A registered office in the UK
Tax ID EIN from the IRS Corporation Tax registration with HMRC
Yearly filings State annual report and federal tax filings Confirmation statement, accounts, and tax return
Often chosen by Sellers and service providers working with US clients and platforms Businesses working with UK and European customers

Some founders register both. Ask us about registering a US LLC and UK LTD together.
