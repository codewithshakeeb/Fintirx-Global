PHYSICAL PRESENCE

Registered Agent and Address

Every US LLC needs a registered agent with a physical address in its state of formation to receive legal and government mail. We provide this statutory service in all 50 states.

In addition, our Plus and Complete packages provide a unique commercial business address with daily mail intake, digital PDF scanning, and private forwarding, ensuring your home address never appears on public state records.

FEDERAL TAX COMPLIANCE

EIN and Federal Taxes

Most US companies need an Employer Identification Number (EIN) to open US bank accounts, register payment processors, and file taxes. We prepare and submit Form SS-4 directly with the IRS for international founders without an SSN (IRS processing for non-US residents typically takes 3–6 weeks).

Owners outside the US have specific federal filing duties, including mandatory annual information returns for 25%+ foreign-owned single-member LLCs (IRS Form 5472 and pro-forma Form 1120). We provide complete filing assistance.
