Formation and business support services

Choose the support your business needs. Each service has its own eligibility checks and an agreed scope, so you only pay for what you use.

Core Service

1. Company Formation

Register a US LLC, US corporation or UK LTD with verified accuracy and complete digital documentation.

What is included:

  • Name reservation & clearance check
  • Articles of Organization or Companies House IN01 lodgement
  • Operating Agreement or Articles of Association template
  • Registered agent or registered office initial setup
  • Immediate digital delivery of certified corporate records

What you will need:

  • Proposed company name(s)
  • Valid government passport or national ID
  • Proof of residential address (utility bill or bank statement)
  • Summary of business activity and ownership breakdown
Important Note: Government filing fees (state Secretary of State fees in the US, Companies House fees in the UK) are charged separately at cost unless itemized in a bundled quote.

Tax IDs

2. EIN and ITIN Support

Tax identification numbers let your company or you personally deal with the US tax system and open merchant accounts.

What is included:

  • EIN application preparation for eligible corporate entities
  • ITIN application preparation for eligible international individuals
  • Thorough review of identity documents prior to submission
  • Direct status follow-up with the Internal Revenue Service

What you will need:

  • Company details & filed Articles of Organization
  • Valid passport with clear photo and expiration
  • Proof of residential address
  • The specific reason and tax exception for requesting an ITIN
Important Note: Government processing times vary. ITIN applications have strict documentation rules. Certified Acceptance Agent (CAA) services, where required, are clearly separated and itemized.

Ongoing Duties

3. Annual Tax and Compliance

Keep your company in good standing every year without unexpected state administrative penalties or dissolution.

What is included (US):

  • State annual report preparation and submission
  • Federal tax return filing support
  • Information returns for foreign-owned LLCs (IRS Form 5472 & 1120)
  • Annual registered agent renewal coordination

What is included (UK):

  • Annual confirmation statement filing with Companies House
  • Statutory annual accounts support
  • Corporation Tax return preparation support (CT600)
  • Quarterly VAT return filing support where registered
Important Note: Complex accounting and tax return certifications are coordinated with licensed accounting professionals. Late filings lead to statutory penalties, so we provide advance reminders.

Application Readiness

4. Banking and Payment Support

Banks and payment providers ask for specific documents and information. We help you prepare them accurately.

What is included:

  • Formation document pack structured for compliance review
  • Commercial proof of address documentation
  • Business model description and commercial activity summary
  • Step-by-step guidance through online onboarding flows
  • Assistance formulating responses to follow-up KYC questions

What you will need:

UK Customs & Tax

5. VAT and EORI Registration

Companies selling to UK customers or moving physical goods across the UK border require these statutory registrations.

What is included:

  • Eligibility and threshold liability review
  • UK VAT registration preparation with HMRC
  • Economic Operators Registration and Identification (EORI) application
  • Initial guidance on filing deadlines and digital VAT accounting

What you will need:

  • UK company incorporation certificate & UTR number
  • Description of physical products and supplier invoices
  • Estimated or historical UK annual turnover
  • UK bank account details for VAT disbursement
Important Note: Whether registration is required depends on turnover thresholds, physical warehousing locations, and movement of goods. We confirm eligibility before initiating filings.

Brand Defense

6. Trademark Registration

Protect your brand name and logo across the United States (USPTO) and United Kingdom (UK IPO).

What is included:

  • Preliminary conflict search for existing registered marks
  • Application preparation with Nice Classification classification
  • Submission coordination with USPTO and/or UK IPO
  • Docket monitoring and examination status updates

What you will need:

  • Exact word mark text or high-resolution logo graphic
  • List of commercial goods and services sold under the mark
  • Specimen of use in commerce (for US in-use filings)
  • Owner or corporate entity legal details
Important Note: Official registration is decided exclusively by the respective government trademark office. Statutory examination fees and publication timelines are separate from our service fee.

State Taxes

9. USA Sales Tax Support

Selling to US consumers may create physical or economic nexus obligations across multiple US states.

What is included:

  • Sales nexus analysis based on trading volume and inventory locations
  • Registration preparation for states where permits are required
  • Guidance on marketplace facilitator laws and exemptions
  • Filing calendar setup for monthly, quarterly, or annual returns

What you will need:

  • State-by-state breakdown of US gross revenue and transaction count
  • Locations of 3PL fulfillment warehouses or Amazon FBA centers
  • Valid US EIN and company formation certificate
Important Note: Nexus rules differ significantly across individual states. Specialized tax filings are coordinated through licensed tax practitioners.
