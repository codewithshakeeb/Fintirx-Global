U.S. LLC vs. UK LTD: Which Structure Fits You?

Based on Section 1 of our Core Business Services Guide.

Feature / Attribute 🇺🇸 United States LLC 🇬🇧 United Kingdom LTD
Governing Authority Secretary of State (State-by-state, e.g., Wyoming, Delaware) Companies House (Single centralized national registry)
Liability Protection Full corporate veil protecting personal assets of members Limited liability by shares protecting shareholders and directors
Tax Classification Pass-through by default (Disregarded entity or Partnership); option for C-Corp Subject to UK Corporation Tax (currently 19%–25%) on net profits
Foreign Owner Reporting IRS Form 5472 + Pro Forma 1120 mandatory for 25%+ foreign owned Annual Statutory Accounts & Company Tax Return (CT600) to HMRC
Annual Maintenance State Annual Report / Franchise Tax + Registered Agent renewal Annual Confirmation Statement + Accounts + Registered Office
Public Privacy High in Wyoming & New Mexico (members not published on state registry) Low (Directors, PSC shareholders, and accounts published on public registry)
Global Merchant Gateways Full access to US Stripe, PayPal, Mercury, Shopify Payments Full access to UK/European Stripe, Wise, Tide, PayPal Europe
Recommended For SaaS, digital creators, international consultants, e-commerce stores European trade, UK market focus, founders wanting quick single-agency setup
