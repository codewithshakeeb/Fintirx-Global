OUR STORY & MISSION

Built for International Founders

Empowering cross-border entrepreneurs in Pakistan and worldwide with honest guidance, verified state filings, and transparent pricing.

Our Story

Fintrix Global was established to solve a critical problem encountered by international entrepreneurs: opaque pricing, unreliable middlemen, and misleading promises regarding foreign bank accounts. We believe cross-border company formation should be transparent, accessible, and grounded in strict statutory compliance.

Headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan, our bilingual specialists guide founders through the exact legal requirements of the United States and United Kingdom. We do not use automated, error-prone filing bots; every application is verified by our compliance desk before submission.

How We Work

1

Consultation

We review your business model, target customers, and ownership structure to recommend the appropriate entity and state.

2

Clear Itemized Quote

You receive an upfront quote that clearly separates our service fees from government filing and registry fees. Zero surprise charges.

3

Agreed Scope

Once approved, we prepare and lodge your filings directly, delivering official corporate certificates and tracking reminders.
